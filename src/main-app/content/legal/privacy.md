## 2. Personal Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Data

When you register for the Service, we collect:

- **Email address** - used as your account identifier and for account-related communications (password resets, security notifications)
- **Password** - stored only in hashed form; we never have access to your plain-text password
- **Google account identifier** - if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive a verified identifier and your email address from Google to create or link your account. We do not receive your Google password, and signing in with Google does not give us access to your Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, or other Google services. We request access to a Google service only when you connect a feature that needs it (see Section 2.8). We keep an encrypted sign-in token (encrypted with keys managed by Google Cloud Key Management Service) only so we can revoke our access at Google when you delete your account.
- **Apple account identifier** - if you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple identifier and your email address (your real address or an Apple Hide My Email relay address) and, on your first sign-in only, the name you choose to share, which we store with your account. We do not receive your Apple password, and signing in with Apple does not give us access to any other Apple data. We keep an encrypted sign-in token (encrypted with keys managed by Google Cloud Key Management Service) only so we can revoke our access at Apple when you delete your account.

### 2.2 User-Generated Content

When you use the Service, you create and store content including:

- Inbox items (stuff), actions, and projects
- Descriptions, comments, and tags
- File attachments (stored in the Reference file manager)
- Photos, voice recordings and files you capture to your Inbox (stored as attachments)
- Recurring action templates
- Calendar entries and scheduling data
- Project brainstorm canvases (the ideas you write on a project's mind map) — stored on our servers, never in your Google Drive, and deleted with the project or your account
- Project templates (a name and the action titles and descriptions you save from a project) — kept until you delete the template or your account
- Horizons of Focus — your areas of focus, goals, vision and purpose & principles, which of your projects you link to an area or goal, and when you last reviewed each level — stored on our servers and deleted with your account

You control what content you create. We process this content solely to provide the Service to you.

The camera and microphone are used only when you choose Photo or Voice, through your browser's permission prompt. Nothing is recorded in the background. Voice recordings are stored as files and are not transcribed.

### 2.3 Inbound Email Content (Email-to-Inbox)

If you use the **Email-to-Inbox** feature (available on the Pro and Team plans), you can generate a unique email address that forwards messages into your inbox as items. When you or others send mail to that address, we process the message content to create an item in your inbox, including:

- The sender's email address
- The subject line and message body
- Any attachments included with the message

This may include personal data about third parties (the people who send mail to your address). We process it solely to create the corresponding item in your inbox. You can pause capture or reset (invalidate) your inbox address at any time from your settings.

### 2.4 Collaboration Data (Team plan)

If you use collaboration features on the Team plan, certain data becomes visible to the other WhatsNextAction users you choose to work with:

- **Connections** - when you invite another person to connect, we store the invitee's email address and send them an invitation. Invitations may be sent to people who do not yet have a WhatsNextAction account.
- **Delegation** - when you delegate an action to a connection, the action's title, description, and related details are shared with that person as an item in their inbox, and completion updates are shared back with you.
- **Shared projects** - when you share a project, the members you choose can see the project's actions (titles, descriptions, attachments), assignments, completion status, and comments. Your personal tags and the area and goal you link a shared project to remain private to you and are never shown to other members.

We share this data only with the specific users you choose, and only to provide the collaboration features you have enabled.

### 2.5 Billing and Payment Data

If you subscribe to a paid plan, we collect and store:

- **Billing details** - the full name, address, and country you enter at checkout (the country determines the VAT rate)
- **Payment records** - date, amount, plan, status, payment method type and, for cards, the card brand and last 4 digits, and refunds of each payment, together with the invoices and credit notes we issue to you

### 2.8 Connected Google Services (Google Calendar Sync and Google Drive Storage)

You can connect Google services to WhatsNextAction. Each service is connected separately, on its own Google consent screen, and you can disconnect each one at any time in your settings.

#### Google Calendar Sync

If you connect **Google Calendar sync** (available on the Pro and Team plans), you grant us permission, on a separate Google consent screen, to create and manage events in your Google Calendar, to see the list of your calendars, and to create a dedicated **WhatsNextAction** calendar in your Google account. We create that calendar only when you ask us to (after connecting, or later in your settings); you can also sync with another calendar you choose. When you connect, we store:

- An encrypted Google refresh token (encrypted with keys managed by Google Cloud Key Management Service), which lets us act for you without asking you to sign in to Google again
- The email address of the connected Google account and the Google permissions you granted
- The timezone of your browser at the moment you connect, used to place timed events at the right time
- The calendar you selected and, if you create one, the identifier of your WhatsNextAction calendar
- For each synced action, the Google event identifier, its sync state and, for an occurrence of a recurring event, the identifier of its series

**What we send to Google:** for each scheduled action in your Next, Today, Calendar and Waiting For lists, its title, description, and scheduled date, time and duration.

**What we read from Google:**

- Changes to the events that WhatsNextAction created in your calendar (for example, a reschedule or a delete), so we can update the matching action. If you delete such an event in Google, we move its action to Trash.
- If you sync with the WhatsNextAction calendar: the events you create in that calendar in Google (their title, description, date, time and duration), which we store as scheduled actions in your account. For a recurring event, each occurrence within the next 30 days is stored as its own action. These actions are then part of your content (Section 2.2) and sync like any other scheduled action.
- The list of calendars you can write to, so you can choose one.

We do not read, import, or store events in your other calendars unless WhatsNextAction created them.

You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time in your settings. Your WhatsNextAction calendar and all events already in your Google Calendar stay there. We keep the identifier of your WhatsNextAction calendar and of your synced events so that reconnecting continues where you left off.

#### Google Drive Storage

If you connect **Google Drive storage** (available on all plans), you grant us permission, on a separate Google consent screen, to create files in your Google Drive and to access only the files that WhatsNextAction created there (the Google `drive.file` permission). We cannot see or open any other files in your Google Drive. When the first file is stored in your Google Drive, we create a visible `WNA` folder there, with `reference` and `attachments` subfolders, and a `trash` subfolder for files you move to the trash in WhatsNextAction; folders you create on the Google Drive side of Reference are created inside `reference`. When you delete such a folder, we move its Google Drive folder to your Google Drive trash. The same encrypted Google refresh token and connected-account details described above are used.

**What we store:** for each file kept in your Google Drive, its name, size, file type, its Google Drive file identifier, and the folder it belongs to. We do not keep a copy of the file contents on our servers: when you upload, download, or preview a file stored in Google Drive, its contents pass through our servers only to be transferred between your device and Google Drive.

**Your Google Drive quota:** files stored in your Google Drive count against your own Google storage quota, not against your WhatsNextAction storage limit.

If you disconnect Google Drive, the files stay in your Google Drive, but you cannot open them in WhatsNextAction until you connect Google Drive again.

If you use the **AI assistant** (available on all plans), it carries out the requests you give it on your content. To provide it, we store:

The content the assistant reads and changes on your instruction is your content described in Section 2.2; it reaches the same content as a connected AI app (Section 2.9). Payments for credit packs are billing and payment data described in Section 2.5.
